Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cognitive Prosthetics an FDA medical device?

No. Cognitive Prosthetics is purposely developed as a non-clinical consumer wellness and workplace productivity wearable. It does not diagnose, treat, or cure medical conditions; rather, it provides behavioral reinforcement and executive function support.

Why a dedicated wearable rather than a smartwatch or phone app?

Smartphones and smartwatches are notorious engines of distraction for neurodivergent brains. Screens overwhelm attention, notifications are easily tuned out, and smartwatches suffer from severe battery drain with continuous sensing. A collar clip provides hands-free, screen-free, discreet haptic feedback right where you need it.
